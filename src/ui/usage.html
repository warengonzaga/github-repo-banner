What these numbers tell you

These are partial observations of this instance. They are not a count of people, installations or projects that depend on the service.

Requests include more than people

Previews, bots and retries can count. Browser, CDN and GitHub image-cache hits never reach this server. Opt-outs, disabled periods and failed writes are excluded.

Repositories are an estimate

Referers may be absent or spoofed. Repository existence and visibility are not verified. Distinct identifiers use HyperLogLog, with about 0.81% standard error.

What is stored

Statistics are optional. When enabled, they store daily page and banner counts and a sketch of hashed repository identifiers. They do not store a project list, banner designs, IPs, cookies or visitor identifiers. Daily keys expire seven days after their last write.

Your choices

The generator’s Exclude this banner from usage statistics checkbox excludes future banner requests. For a page request, add ?stats=false to its URL; this choice is not carried through navigation. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control request headers are also respected. These choices cannot undo counts already recorded.

Image-search caching and request limits still operate when statistics are off. Hosting providers may keep separate access logs.
