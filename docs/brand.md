# Brand guidelines

GitHub Repo Banner's icon is a notebook with a wide rectangle inside it: the notebook represents a README, and the rectangle represents the banner placed in that README.

Coral highlights the banner and relates to the app's existing coral accent. Ink provides a neutral frame. The colour choice is about visual emphasis and consistency, rather than a symbolic meaning attached to orange.

## Palette

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coral | `#E85D32` | 232, 93, 50 | The banner inside the notebook |
| Ink | `#0D1117` | 13, 17, 23 | Notebook and lettering on light backgrounds; app-icon tile |
| Soft white | `#F3F4F6` | 243, 244, 246 | Notebook and lettering on dark backgrounds |

The coral banner keeps the same colour across light and dark versions. White and black one-colour assets use `#FFFFFF` and `#000000` respectively.

Coral has approximately 3.48:1 contrast against white and 5.45:1 against ink. It is an accent for the logo; use a separately checked colour for small interface text.

These are digital RGB colours. For print, ask the printer to convert and proof them for the intended paper and process. No Pantone match is specified.

## Backgrounds

- On white or light surfaces, use an ink notebook with a coral banner.
- On dark surfaces, use the supplied soft-white notebook with a coral banner. Its inner edges are slightly thinner to balance its apparent weight.
- For one-colour reproduction, use the supplied black, white or coral variant. Keep the open page area transparent.
- On photography or busy patterns, place the logo on a clear, solid background.

SVG is preferred for responsive layouts. Use a PNG when the destination does not support SVG, and choose an export at least as large as its intended display size.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

Leave clear space equal to **one banner height** around every side of the visible logo. This is 32 units on the standard symbol's 256-unit canvas; scale it with the logo. The file's built-in padding does not replace this spacing rule.

| Artwork | Minimum screen size |
| --- | --- |
| Symbol | 16 px; use the [small light](../assets/brand/svg/symbol-small-color.svg) or [small dark](../assets/brand/svg/symbol-small-dark.svg) drawing below 32 px |
| Horizontal logo | 240 px wide |
| Stacked logo | 160 px wide |

Use the symbol when the name would become too small. Do not shrink the full wordmark into a favicon. The [web assets](../assets/brand/README.md#web-integration) include a dedicated small-size favicon and icons with built-in background tiles.

These are screen recommendations. For print, inspect a proof at the final size to ensure the binding and banner remain separate.

## Typography and attribution

The horizontal and stacked logos use Liberation Sans Regular for “GitHub” and Liberation Sans Bold for “Repo Banner,” with slightly tightened spacing. The single-line wordmark uses Liberation Sans Bold throughout.

All logo lettering is converted to vector outlines, so no font installation is needed. Use the supplied artwork instead of retyping or rearranging it. The font's SIL Open Font License 1.1 and attribution are included in [FONT-LICENSE.txt](../assets/brand/FONT-LICENSE.txt).

## Keep the logo consistent

Preserve the geometry, orientation and approved colours. Avoid stretching, rotation, added shadows, gradients or outlines; moving or resizing the inset banner; closing the open page area; and rearranging the name within a supplied layout.

The notebook and banner should remain the two defining elements. Use the provided small-size drawing when space is tight, rather than removing or redrawing parts of the icon.
